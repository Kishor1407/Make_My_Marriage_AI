# Stitch Design System: Coastal Editorial SaaS

**Project:** Make My Marriage Platform  
**Stitch Project ID:** `9312812799714425334`  
**Theme:** Coastal Editorial SaaS (Warm Coastal Stone + Deep Indigo/Teal Slate)  

---

## 1. Brand Philosophy
This design system merges the operational rigor of modern enterprise SaaS with the editorial elegance of high-end wedding curation:
- **Clean Architectural White & Sand Foundation:** High-utility layout inspired by pristine galleries and warm travertine stone.
- **Precision SaaS Execution:** Crisp hairline grid lines, high-utility data density, and frictionless guest management workflows.
- **Tactile Restraint:** Restrained micro-interactions, subtle glassmorphism on floating surfaces, and structured component frames.
